# Meʻa Hoʻokipa Dining Operations v18.3

## Employee Operations Access, Blueprint Editing, Breakout Inventory

### Employee accounts get full Operations access
`role === "employee"` (the shared per-venue accounts like `haleohana.e@`) now
sees every daily operations data-entry tab — Cloths, Leis, Gelato/Ice Cream,
Pax, and the new Breakout tab — not just Assignments. History stays
management-only, matching the audit-trail convention every other role
already follows.

### Blueprint move, resize, and center (Designer tab)
Imported blueprint tracing images could previously only be shown/hidden and
faded — no way to reposition them, which made them nearly unusable on the
huge Gateway/Aloha canvases where the image's native size never matched the
workspace. Added:
- A **Move & resize** toggle — while active, drag the image to move it or
  drag its corner handle to resize.
- **Center** and **Fit to canvas** one-click buttons.
- New imports now default to fitting the current canvas instead of
  stretching to fill it edge-to-edge regardless of aspect ratio.

### Breakout / inventory tab
New Operations tab tracking non-gelato consumables and supplies, organized
into area sub-tabs: Drink Station, Ice-Cream Station (supply items only —
the ice cream flavors themselves stay in the dedicated Gelato tab), Keiki
Station, Back Area (Janitorial), and Back Area (Non-Food Cost). Per item:
stock quantity, a free-text unit (case/box/BIB size/etc.), and order
quantity. Managers can add items to an area, set an optional code/SKU, and
deactivate items without losing history.

### Gelato: freezer stock + reorder highlight
- Added a **freezer stock (backup)** field per flavor, separate from the
  station-level tub tracking, for additional/new-order or old stock kept in
  the freezer rather than at the station.
- Any flavor with status **Almost Out** or **Empty** now gets a red
  highlight on its card, an "Order" badge, and a summary banner at the top
  of the tab counting how many flavors currently need ordering.

### Firebase
No new top-level paths. `dailyOperations/{date}` records gained a
`breakout` field (see `FIREBASE_V18.1_SETUP.md`, which was also corrected
this round — the account-linked assignment picker and its `users` list-read
rule were removed in v18.2 but the doc hadn't caught up until now).
